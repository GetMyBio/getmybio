# GetMy.Bio

The free link-in-bio and digital biography page.

Each person gets one clean page at `getmy.bio/@handle` that holds their story, photo, work, links, social profiles, crypto wallets, and a scannable QR code — an alternative to Linktree that centers a full biography instead of a bare list of links.

## The idea

GetMy.Bio turns a physical "scan to connect" habit into a digital identity: a single QR code opens a person's whole profile. It's a utility for sharing your biography, not another social network — there is no feed.

## Features

- **One page per person** — your story, photo, work, links, socials, and wallets, all at `getmy.bio/@handle`.
- **QR-code-first** — scan to open someone's biography. The modern "get my bio" link.
- **A real biography, not a link list** — profession templates, education, projects, books, music, and more.
- **AI "Digital Double"** — your page answers visitors' questions on your behalf.
- **Fan pages** — rich, SEO-friendly pages for notable people and brands.
- **Karma + leaderboard** — earn Karma by sharing; redeem it for premium features.
- **Who-viewed-you insights** — see who's visited your page.
- **Crypto wallets** — list your wallets for tips and payments.
- **Markdown-lite rich bio** — bold, italic, headings, and links.

## Why GetMy.Bio

A link-in-bio tool gives you a list of links. GetMy.Bio gives you a biography — your whole story on one page, shareable anywhere through a single QR code.

## Free forever

Creating a profile is free. Premium unlocks themes, analytics, and a verified badge — and can also be earned through Karma.

## Built with

Raw PHP + JSON flat-file (no database), served with a PWA manifest and service worker.
